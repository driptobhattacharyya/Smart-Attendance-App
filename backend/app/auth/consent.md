Your employer uses Attendify to record when you are at work. This page explains what the app collects and why, in plain words. You can read it in under a minute.

## What we collect

- **Your location, only while you are checked in.** The app checks whether your phone is inside the office area. It does not track you before you check in or after you check out.
- **Bluetooth signal strength** from your desk beacon (a small tag at your desk). It tells us whether you are at your desk. We do not collect anything else your phone can hear.
- **A face embedding.** This is a list of numbers calculated from your photo. {face_processing}
- **Timestamps:** when you check in, check out, and when the app last reported in.

{desk_app_section}## Why

To work out how long you were actually present at work. Time only counts while you are inside the office area and near your desk beacon. The face check confirms that it is really you checking in.

## Who can see it

Your managers and the administrators of your company. Other employees cannot see your data.

## How long we keep it

Detailed location and signal records are deleted after {retention_days} days. Your daily totals (the dates and minutes worked) are kept after that.

## Your choices

- You can **withdraw your consent** at any time in the app. Tracking stops straight away and you are checked out. You will not be able to check in again until you agree again.
- You can **request a copy of your data** in the app at any time, including after you withdraw.
- Questions or other requests about your data should go to your employer, who is responsible for it.
